# About

Shulker is a command-line tool that keeps a Minecraft modpack in one file, `shulker.json`, and does everything from there. It fetches mods from Modrinth and CurseForge, locks exact versions and hashes, builds a client or a server, links the client into a launcher, and exports a `.mrpack` or CurseForge pack.

Shulker is made by [Andrew Mast](https://github.com/AndrewMast). Its source lives at [github.com/shulker-sh/shulker](https://github.com/shulker-sh/shulker), and bugs and requests go in its [issues](https://github.com/shulker-sh/shulker/issues).
